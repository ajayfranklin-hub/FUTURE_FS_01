Our Story

Started by people who missed home filter coffee.

Karai Café opened on Fort Road in 2021, a stone's throw from the Sivagangai Fort and the Rajarajeshwari Amman Temple inside it. The name means "shore" — a nod to the old tanks and lakes that this part of the district was built around.

We brew filter coffee the way it's meant to be — decoction simmered slow, poured tumbler-to-davara until the froth sits right — and pair it with a short, honest menu of Chettinad-leaning tiffin: idli, dosai, kara chutney, and a few snacks that don't show up on every menu in town.

Most mornings you'll find pilgrims heading to the temple, government office staff on a quick break, and students from the nearby colleges sharing the same five tables. That mix is exactly what we built this place for.
